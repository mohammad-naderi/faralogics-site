About FARA

Founded in Toronto in 1995, FARA Logics Inc. is a technology consulting firm specializing in enterprise architecture, software engineering, and technology strategy.

Its work spans complex enterprise information systems, systems integration, enterprise application security, and the architectural relationships between business capabilities and technology.

Over the years, FARA has brought together experienced technology professionals, with an emphasis on sound architectural principles, open standards, independent technical judgment, and the professional development of its people.

Our Approach

FARA's approach is grounded in careful analysis, architectural clarity, and engineering discipline.

Technology decisions are considered in relation to the larger systems of which they form a part: their underlying structures, their relationship to business capabilities, their interoperability, security, and their capacity to evolve over time.

Equally important is the sharing of knowledge, the development of professional expertise, and the cultivation of independent technical judgment.
